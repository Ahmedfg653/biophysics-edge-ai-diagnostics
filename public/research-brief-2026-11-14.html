Reviewer Question Audited Scientific & Engineering Answer Evidence Status
1. What is the specific scientific problem? Smartphone camera remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) suffers severe signal corruption under hand/head motion, optical defocus blur, and ambient illumination shifts—causing unconstrained estimators to output confident but physiologically invalid heart-rate (HR) estimates. IMPLEMENTED
2. What is the new hypothesis / contribution? Enforcing a two-stage Uncertainty-Aware Selective Abstention Gate—combining Laplacian focus variance Var(∇²I) ≥ τ_focus and composite Signal Quality Index SQI ≥ τ_SQI—reduces Selective Risk (HR MAE in BPM) at an explicit Coverage trade-off within a 16KB page-aligned C++17/Kotlin on-device pipeline. PROPOSED

(a) Deterministic synthetic signal generators and unit-tested mathematical functions in src/utils/biophysicsMath.ts, (b) interactive Selective Prediction Risk–Coverage & signal-quality laboratories, and (c) complete reference C++17 NDK / Kotlin architectural blueprints (64-byte cache-aligned structs with static_assert, reflection-padded filtfilt, local 4-timestamp clock-offset estimator).
